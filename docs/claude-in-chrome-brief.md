**What the site is**
A free, independent consumer guide for Indianapolis-metro homeowners (Indianapolis, Carmel, Fishers, Westfield, Noblesville, Greenwood, Franklin, Avon, Brownsburg, Plainfield) with foundation, basement, crawl space or mold problems. We are NOT a contractor. We explain problems in plain English, show real Indianapolis repair costs, and compare local contractors using only public, checkable data (Google reviews, BBB rating, years in business, licensing, ownership). Contractors can't pay to change their ranking. Some contractors will pay per lead; that's disclosed on every contractor list and on /disclosures.

**SEO comes first.** One H1 per page, H2 for sections, H3 inside them. Short, clean, lowercase URLs exactly as listed below (set the page slug and parent page). Fast and mobile-first: no sliders, carousels, video backgrounds, pop-ups or heavy animation; no text inside images. Descriptive link text, image alt text, breadcrumbs, and "By [Your name] · Last updated [date]" under the H1 of every guide.

**Menu:** Problems ▾ · Services ▾ · Costs · Contractors ▾ · How We Rank · button "Get Free Quotes" → /get-a-quote
**Footer:** About, How We Rank, Disclosures, Contact, Privacy Policy, Areas We Serve, News
