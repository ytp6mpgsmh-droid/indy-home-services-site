## Authorship rules

* One real person, real name, real photo. Never invent team members, credentials or years of experience.
* Byline on every guide: "By [Your name] · Reviewed by [Engineer/Inspector name, credential] · Last updated [date]". Show "Reviewed by" only after a real review has happened.
* About page covers: who you are and why you started the site; what the site is and isn't (a guide, not a contractor); how content is made (research, sources, AI-assisted drafting with every fact checked by you); how to report an error.
* Google's guidance is to make clear who created content and how. Being open about AI-assisted drafting is fine; publishing unchecked facts is not.

## Disclosure (draft; have the attorney confirm wording)

Short version (footer + top of every contractor list):

> **How we make money:** Some contractors pay us when a homeowner contacts them through this site. That never changes their ranking, which is based only on public data. [Learn more](/disclosures)

Full Disclosures page:

> IndyFoundationGuide.com is free for homeowners. We earn money when participating contractors pay a fee for homeowner requests sent through our forms or tracked phone numbers. Contractors who pay us are labeled "Participating contractor."
>
> Payment never affects rankings, ratings, reviews or what we write. Rankings use only public, checkable information (Google reviews, BBB rating, years in business, licensing), and our method is published on our How We Rank page. Contractors can't pay to be listed, removed or moved up. We include highly rated companies that don't pay us.
>
> We are not a contractor, engineer or inspector, and our guides are general information, not professional advice for your home. Questions or corrections: [email].
